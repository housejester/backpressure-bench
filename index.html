Model, not measurement: a 150M-row, 50-column source (~25 GB of Arrow, ~5.5 GB of Parquet) read at ~200 MB/s and written to object storage at ~25 MB/s (or to local disk at ~250 MB/s, where the file streams instead of being held — the same write measured 149 MiB locally against 2979 MiB to GCS), on a pod with a ~2.5 GiB resident baseline and an 8 GiB limit. Rates and thresholds are set so the behaviour matches what was measured; the numbers on the toggles are the real ones. Both consumer-side bounds are still live in production; the read-ahead bound is the one that closes the gap.
